The Process

Four Steps, No Surprises

We make selling your car simple, transparent, and worth your time.

  1. 01

    Tell Us About the Car

    Year, make, model, mileage — and the VIN if you have it to hand. Two minutes, no obligation, and nothing leaves our acquisition desk.

  2. 02

    Receive a Firm Offer

    A specialist who knows the marque prices the car against live retail, not a generic book value, and comes back the same business day with a number that stands as written.

  3. 03

    Accept and Sign

    Title work, lien payoff, and DMV paperwork are handled in house. If the car is still financed, we settle with your bank directly.

  4. 04

    Get Paid, We Collect

    Funds go out by wire or check on signature. Enclosed, insured transport collects the car door to door, anywhere in the country, at our cost.
