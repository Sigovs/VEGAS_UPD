2024 Lamborghini Urus S

Stock #86105X · VIN ZPBUB3ZL9RLA35134 · 9,956 miles

The super-SUV that started it all — now with even more attitude.

Vegas Auto Gallery presents a 2024 Lamborghini Urus S finished in the striking Blu Cepheus Metallic over a Nero Ade and Bianco Leda leather interior. With just 9,956 miles, Stock #86105X, VIN ZPBUB3ZL9RLA35134, this example pairs everyday usability with genuine Lamborghini theatre.

Performance

At its heart is a 4.0-liter twin-turbocharged V8 producing 657 horsepower, paired with an eight-speed automatic and Lamborghini's permanent all-wheel-drive system with active torque vectoring. The result is supercar acceleration in a vehicle that seats the whole family — 0–60 in the low three-second range and a top speed north of 190 mph.

Design

The Urus S sharpens the formula with darkened exterior accents, a carbon-fiber detailing package, and an aggressive front fascia. Inside, the dual-tone Nero Ade and Bianco Leda cabin is trimmed in fine leather with contrast stitching, hexagonal motifs, and Lamborghini's signature flight-deck control layout.

Technology & Comfort

Dual touchscreen infotainment with navigation and Apple CarPlay, a head-up display, 360° camera, adaptive air suspension, and a premium sound system come together with heated and ventilated front seats for a cockpit that is as comfortable on the Strip as it is on track.
